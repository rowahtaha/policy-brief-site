This is where I'll write longer, more personal takes on tech policy, privacy, and digital rights — pieces that don't fit the weekly newsletter format, but that I still want on the record.

Expect commentary on the stories behind the stories: why a particular enforcement action matters more than it looks, what a new law actually changes versus what it just claims to change, and where I think the field of tech policy is getting something wrong.

If you want the fast-moving weekly roundup, that's the [Newsletter](../newsletter.html). This is the slower, more opinionated companion to it.
