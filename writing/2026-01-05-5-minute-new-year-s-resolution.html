5-minute New Year’s resolution

Rowa Taha · January 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Happy New year! Hope this year brings peace, prosperity, and ZERO scammers in your inbox.

I’m inviting you all to take 5 mins to leave all thoes old, dusty WhatsApp groups that you never use. Seriously, ghost them! Your number and any info about you is just sitting there waiting to be used to scam you. Please do the same for your parents and grandparents!!! And ofc your kids. If they aren’t talking in the group, they shouldn’t be in the group.

Bonus: You’ll get a lot of storage space back from deleting all those “Good Morning” GIFs from 2022. Win-win. Guys this is actually unreal. I used to save 50GB of WhatsApp storage. Down to 20!!🙂‍↔️

Soo.. My grandad recently got scammed out of $3k. The scammer pretended to be someone he knows and gave a very sentimental private story. Then bam! Money’s gone. We are still at early stages of digital literacy gloablly and it’s getting scary out there.

Today, for the second time, I got a call from someone saying there’s a “conference today” and he wants to send me a link to join on zoom. Many students on the Duke grad WhatsApp/GroupMe groups got the same. Everyone knows never to open a random link, but these “cybersquatters” are getting sneaky with URLs that look 99% real.

And now we have Deepfakes. AI can now clone voices so accurately it’s scary. If you get a call that sounds EXACTLY like your mom or your best friend asking for money, HANG UP. Call them back on their actual number to verify. Always double check. Don’t let the AI “emotions” trick you!

This is a threat to national security, but WE CAN HELP EACHOTHER a lot by spreading the word! Start with 5 mins for your your mum/aunnt/grandad and take them out of all the groups they’re not on mych. I know there is probably an app for this but please don’t give into capitalism! Do it yourself, you never know they’re not taking the data for themselves 🧐ifyky.

Social innovation can be a way to make technology beneficial for all thru civic engagement. Technology has systematically made societies more and more miserable. We know of the dangers of global warming, plastics, sugar… so many studies and dangers! Yet companies profit off you for years only for you to discover they have serious effects much later. You might get some cash in a class actions lawsuit, but it’s nothing compared to the billions they made off of you.

Prevention is better than the cure 😌.

Have a blessed year
