Digital Rights of Oceania

Oceania spans a huge stretch of ocean but a tiny fraction of the world's people, from Australia and New Zealand's mature privacy regulators to Pacific island nations with a few hundred thousand citizens, dial-up-era connectivity, and no dedicated data protection law on the books at all.

This project answers the same twenty questions used across the Digital Rights Atlas for all 14 countries of Oceania, covering data privacy, artificial intelligence, surveillance, and free expression online. Built from real statutes, official regulator guidance, and reporting from groups such as Freedom House, the Committee to Protect Journalists, and ARTICLE19, so you can compare what is protected on paper against what actually happens in practice.
