# Story Log — The Rowa Report

Running record of every story that's appeared in the newsletter, the Story Bank,
or Rowa's Thoughts, so future weekly research doesn't repeat one without
realizing it. Check this **before** finalizing a story list — search it by
country, company, or law name, not just headline (a story can repeat under a
totally different headline).

A repeat isn't always wrong — a law that was "proposed" in one issue and
"passed" three months later is a legitimate follow-up, not a duplicate. When
that happens, use it, but say explicitly in the story that it's a follow-up to
issue N, and don't re-explain the whole backstory from scratch.

**How to update this file:** every time an issue or blog post actually
publishes, add its rows here in the same pass (same commit). For the weekly
20-story research pool, log the picked-10 as "Published" and move the other 10
into the Story Bank section — don't log speculative/rejected leads that never
got written up.
