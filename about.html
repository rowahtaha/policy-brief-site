About

Rowa Taha is the creator of The Digital Divide. She holds an LL.M. from American University Washington College of Law and researches and writes about tech policy, privacy law, and AI governance.

She built the Digital Rights Atlas — a 20-question, fully cited breakdown of digital rights and privacy law across 195 countries — and writes The Rowa Report, a weekly newsletter on tech policy news.
