Digital Rights of Africa

Africa is the source. Africa is showing the fastest demographic growth in the world, and more than 60 percent of that population is youth, much of it already online.

This mini project is my attempt at raising awareness on tech policy, so Africans can know their rights and use technology in a safe, informed way. Each country gets a four-card set, with the national flag as the backdrop. Download the set in order and upload it as a PDF or image carousel.
